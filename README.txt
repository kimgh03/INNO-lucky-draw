Simple program requirements

When a guest scans the QR code, they should enter the site.
The site should include a clear UI that opens the INNO Hostel Instagram page:
https://www.instagram.com/innohostel?utm_source=ig_web_button_share_sheet&stkn=ZDNlZDc0MzIxNw==

The page should say that guests can receive a free drink coupon after following
INNO Hostel on Instagram. The design can be simple, with a white background and
INNO Hostel shown clearly at the top.

After the guest follows the account, they should get one lucky draw chance for a
free drink coupon. Because the website cannot reliably confirm Instagram follow
status automatically, the page should include a staff confirmation button. Once
staff presses "Yes", the guest gets one lucky draw chance.

The lucky draw should spin like a roulette wheel and award one of these drinks:
draft beer 450ml, draft beer 270ml, melon sour, purple rain, or jin tonic.
